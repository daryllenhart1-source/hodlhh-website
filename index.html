Care for the whole person

Health is more than a diagnosis. Our nurses look at the full picture: physical needs, daily routines, comfort, and the people who matter most to you.

For workers and families covered by EEOICPA and RECA, we bring that care home, so you can stay where you feel most like yourself.

Who we serve

We care for EEOICPA (U.S. Department of Labor, DEEOIC) and RECA beneficiaries in Northern Utah and along the Wasatch Front, including former nuclear weapons and uranium workers and their families.

In-home care under this program requires an approved EEOICPA medical benefits card, often called the "White Card." Not sure where you stand? Give us a call and we can help explain the process.
